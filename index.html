Lexy

Record a voice note, get a transcript, and keep your thoughts organized. Lexy is a voice notes app for Android.

What is Lexy?

Lexy turns what you say into notes you can search and share. Tap record, speak, and Lexy saves the recording on your phone right away, so a note is never lost because the network dropped. If you allow AI transcription, Lexy writes out what you said in the background.

Features

Record anywhere

Recordings are saved on your device first. Recording works offline and without transcription.

AI transcripts

With your permission, recordings are transcribed automatically. Retry or redo a transcript in another spoken language.

Stay organized

Search and sort your notes, group them into categories and edit the text whenever you like.

Share easily

Send a note as plain text or as a PDF through your phone's share sheet.

Your privacy

Your notes are private. Recordings are only sent for transcription after you agree, note text and audio are never shared with advertisers, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
